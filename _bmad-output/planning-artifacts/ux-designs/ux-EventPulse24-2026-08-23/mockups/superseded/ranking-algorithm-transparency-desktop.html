How Pulse ranks your events

Every Pulse Card shows a 1–5 Confidence Pulse. The dots reflect how strongly this event matches your personal relevance score. The formula and weights below are public and locked — you can compute the Confidence Pulse for any event given its inputs.

Formula

relevance_score = 0.40 × preference_graph_match + 0.25 × source_reliability + 0.20 × recency_fit + 0.15 × novelty_vs_exploration

Component weights

preference_graph_match · 40% source_reliability · 25% recency_fit · 20% novelty_vs_exploration · 15%
Component Weight What it measures
preference_graph_match 0.40 How well this event fits your behavior-derived Preference Graph (dwell, saves, swipes, dismissals). Cold-start fallback: Bayesian-smoothed popularity.
source_reliability 0.25 Per-Source reliability score (β-binomial, prior α=5 β=5). Drops ranking, never removes a source.
recency_fit 0.20 How well the event's start time aligns with your typical engagement windows. Freshness boost: ≤24h ×1.5, 1–3d ×1.2, ≥3d ×1.0.
novelty_vs_exploration 0.15 Balances familiarity (similar to past saves) against exploration (outside usual categories). Serendipity card always gets this boost.

How scores map to dots

The Confidence Pulse on each Pulse Card uses these bucket boundaries. A change in relevance_score that doesn't cross a bucket does NOT change the displayed dots (no-flicker rule, ±0.02 hysteresis).

Our pledge on ranking

  • ✓ No A/B testing on live users. All weight experimentation happens in shadow composition, off the user's screen.
  • ✓ No hidden ranking. The formula and weights are public. They are the PRD, not a secret.
  • ✓ No dark patterns. No "trending now" boards. No urgency badges. No "Don't miss out!"
  • ✓ Calm > engagement. The business model is misaligned with the engagement metric — and that's the moat.
