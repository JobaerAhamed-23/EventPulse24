Delete your account?

We'll permanently delete your account, personal events, saved events, and preference graph within 30 days.

Anonymized aggregates (e.g., "47 events were saved in NYC on Aug 27") survive. We can't reverse deletion.

You can cancel anytime during the 30-day grace from this page or from the email we send.

Case-sensitive. The button activates once this matches.

Variants

1. Enabled primary .btn-primary — after typing DELETE

input.value === "DELETE" (case-sensitive)
  → .btn-primary enabled, terracotta fill, color.ink.inverse label
  → aria-disabled="false"

2. Disabled primary .btn-primary:disabled — initial state

input.value !== "DELETE" (or empty)
  → .btn-primary disabled, color.surface.inset fill, color.ink.faint label
  → aria-disabled="true"
  → cursor: not-allowed

3. Cancel .btn-secondary

Tap → modal closes, focus returns to "Delete account →" button in #danger-zone
  → No state change, no confirmation toast
  → 30-day timer NOT started
