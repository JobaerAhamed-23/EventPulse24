EventPulse24 enters a red ocean dominated by Eventbrite (tickets), Meetup (community), Facebook Events (social), Google/Apple Calendar (passive storage). ## ⚔️ THE FOUR ACTIONS

### 🚫 ELIMINATE — Factors the industry takes for granted that should be removed

| Factor to Eliminate | Why Industry Keeps It | Why EventPulse24 Should Eliminate It |
|---|---|---|
| **Ticketing & payment infrastructure** | Revenue stream for Eventbrite | Capital-heavy, low-margin, distracts from intelligence. Users *hate* paying ticket fees; removing us from this race wins trust. Affiliate to Eventbrite/Ticketmaster instead. |
| **Push notifications as primary communication** | Engagement metric (DAU/MAU) | Aligns business model against user. Users actively *hate* event-app notifications. Build *against* this metric; success = fewer, better notifications. |
| **Social features & friend activity feeds** | Network effect retention | Engagement-trap; reinforces FOMO. Compete on intelligence, not social. |
| **Event creation UI for organizers** | Required for ticketing platforms | Not our job. We're a *consumer* intelligence layer, not a creation tool. |
| **Long onboarding "interest quizzes"** | Industry default; "feels like personalization" | The #1 drop-off point. Replace with calendar connect + swipe (3 swipes, max). |
| **Algorithmic ranking/leaderboards** | Maximizes time-on-app | Drives *irrelevance*; users hate feeling manipulated. Use transparency over ranking. |
| **Ad-supported revenue model** | Default for free apps | Direct conflict with "calm" positioning. Calmer ≠ ad-funded. |
| **Real-time "trending now" carousels** | Implied urgency | Manufactures FOMO. Reposition as "what's worth your time" instead. |
| **Account-required browsing** | Default to grow email lists | Show value before signup ("Pulse Preview" pattern). |

**Strategic value of elimination**: Every factor we eliminate shrinks the cost structure and sharpens positioning. We are NOT a ticketing app, NOT a social app, NOT a creation tool. We are an **intelligence layer**.
