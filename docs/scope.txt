I want to build an AI-powered event discovery and notification website that helps users stay informed about upcoming events based on their interests, location, and preferences. The platform will cover different types of events, including global events, national and local events, and personal events. Global events could include sports tournaments, technology conferences, international events, and other major events, while national and local events could include government events, cultural programs, local sports, meetups, and community activities. Users will also be able to create their own personal events, such as meetings, appointments, birthdays, weddings, get-togethers, and other important activities.

The system will automatically collect and discover events from different websites and online sources using web crawling and AI. AI will identify relevant event information, categorize the events, remove duplicate events, and create structured event records in the system. Users will also be able to create and manage events manually. Each user can customize their interests and notification preferences, such as receiving reminders 5 days, 2 days, 1 day, 1 hour, or at other selected times before an event. Based on these preferences, the system will provide personalized notifications and recommendations, helping users stay aware of the events that are most relevant and important to them.
