BMAD Evaluation 03 / 03 — Challenges & Resolutions

What changed — and how BMAD caught it

Key pivots discovered through the BMAD process that the original scope could not have anticipated

Challenge 01
🌍
Scope was US-centric — missed the real user base
Discovered in: brain-pre-prd-gaps-2026-08-19
The initial scope.txt assumed a US-centric product (Eventbrite, Meetup, NYC/SF beachhead). The brainstorming session revealed the actual user context required a Bangladesh-first launch with completely different infrastructure, language, and payment needs.
✓ Resolution
  • Pivoted to BD-primary launch (Dhaka focus)
  • Added Bangla language support from day 1
  • bKash + Nagad payment integration for Pro tier
  • Dual persona: Maya (US) + Rumi (BD Dhaka professional)
  • BD event sources: events.com.bd, FB groups, university calendars
BMAD Skill bmad-brainstorming — Pre-PRD Gap Fill session
Challenge 02
⚖️
Web crawling treated as a feature, not a legal cliff
Discovered in: brainstorm-intent.md — Gap L1
The brief mentioned crawling as a "risk" but treated it as a core feature. The gap-fill brainstorm identified that this was fundamentally wrong — crawling is optional. Building it as MVP would expose the product to legal liability before it had any users.
✓ Resolution
  • MVP flipped to official APIs only — Eventbrite, PredictHQ, Bandsintown, Songkick
  • RSS feeds from tourism boards & universities as secondary
  • Web crawling deferred to Phase 2 with per-source legal review
  • Partnership deals scoped for Year 2 (venues send data directly)
  • US-only for MVP; EU deferred until legal review complete
BMAD Skill bmad-brainstorming — Gap L1: Legal & Compliance
Challenge 03
🧪
Core product bets were assumptions, not validated facts
Discovered in: brainstorm-intent.md — Gaps V1–V5
The brief assumed the 3-card limit, "Why this?" micro-tag, and Maya persona were correct. None were validated. Building a PRD on unvalidated bets risked 2+ months of rework if even one bet failed in user testing.
✓ Resolution
  • Defined a 1-week, ~$300 validation sprint before PRD freeze
  • 5 user interviews with BD + US knowledge workers ($25 gift cards)
  • Fake-door A/B mockup to test 3-card vs 10-card preference
  • "Why this?" mapped to GDPR Art 22 compliance — dual purpose unlocked
  • Van Westendorp survey for BD WTP ($1–3 USD vs US $5–8)
BMAD Skill bmad-brainstorming — Gap V: User Validation Sprint
BMAD surfaced all three pivots before any code was written — saving significant rework and de-risking the product fundamentally.
